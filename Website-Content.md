### 3. How It Works Section

#### V1 Content

**Eyebrow:** How It Works

**Headline:** From Chaos to Clarity. / In Three Steps.

**Description:** We've refined our process through hundreds of engagements. Here's how we transform your design-to-code workflow.

**Step 01: Discovery & Audit** (Duration: 1-2 weeks)
> We start by understanding your current state. What exists? What's broken? What does your team actually need? We audit your design files, codebase, and workflows to identify gaps and opportunities.

**Step 02: Build & Deliver** (Duration: 2-4 weeks)
> Our team gets to work. Human expertise drives strategy and quality. AI accelerates execution. You get production-ready components in both Figma and code, complete with documentation.

**Step 03: Integrate & Evolve** (Duration: Ongoing)
> We don't just hand off files and disappear. We work with your team to integrate the system, train your engineers, and establish governance. Optional ongoing maintenance keeps everything aligned.
